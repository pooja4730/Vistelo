PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Vistelo is an academic prototype developed for a student Data Science project. The dashboard uses survey data supplied for the CSMVS visitor study. The published prototype is intended to display aggregated insights and does not intentionally expose respondent email addresses or other direct identifiers.

Data used

The prototype may use survey responses for aggregate analysis of visitor demographics, preferences, reported crowding and visitor issues. Recommendations are based on patterns in the project dataset and are not live crowd predictions.

Contact

For project-related questions, contact the student project team through the institution's approved academic channel.
